How it works

From idea to
inbox in ten minutes.

1

Design it

Upload your own artwork or pick a layout. Title, date, time, place, notes, plus-one toggle — all editable in one panel.

2

List them

Type names in or paste a spreadsheet. Email, phone, or both. We flag duplicates before you send.

3

Send it

Everyone at once, or a careful few at a time. Track opens, replies and headcount as they roll in.
