01 / Process

Three steps. No spreadsheet.

Step 01

Design the card

Upload your own artwork or start from a layout. Set the title, date, time, location, notes, and whether plus-ones are welcome.

Step 02

Build the guest list

Add names with an email address, a phone number, or both. Paste a spreadsheet and we map the columns for you.

Step 03

Send and track

Deliver to the full list at once or one guest at a time. Replies arrive in the dashboard and, if you like, your inbox.

02 / Features

What's included.

A.01

Mass send or individual

One click for the whole list, or hand-pick a few. Every guest gets a private RSVP link tied to their name.

A.02

Guest list management

Name, email, phone. CSV import, duplicate detection, household grouping, inline editing.

A.03

Live RSVP tracking

Attending, declined, awaiting. Running headcount, plus-one names, dietary notes, CSV export.

A.04

Email notifications

Instant on each reply, a daily digest, or nothing at all until you go looking.

A.05

Registry links

Amazon, Babylist, or any URL. Shown on the invitation, not buried in a group chat.

A.06

Your own artwork

Upload a finished design at full resolution, or place text over one of ours. Drag-and-drop editor in progress.
