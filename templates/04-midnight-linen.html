How it works

Three movements.

I

Design the card

Upload your own artwork or begin with one of ours. Title, date, place, notes, and a plus-one option if you'd like one.

II

Assemble the list

Names with an email, a phone number, or both. Paste a spreadsheet and we'll sort the columns out for you.

III

Send and receive

Deliver to all at once or one at a time. Replies arrive in your dashboard and, if you wish, your inbox.
